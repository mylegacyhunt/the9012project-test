Explore 90:12

90:12 helps you notice your days, keep the stories that matter, and pass on what life has taught you. Here is how its spaces fit together.

New here? Start with one moment.

Open your Shelf, choose + Waymark, and write something you want to remember. Save it when you are ready. You can explore the other spaces as your story grows.

01

Your life, in perspective

  • The Shelf

    Number Your Days

    See your life one week at a time. Your Shelf is home base: choose a week, remember what matters, and return here whenever you need your bearings.

    Start here, then write one Waymark.

02

Capture life & choose your direction

You do not have to organize a whole lifetime. Begin with what is on your heart today.

  • Waymarks

    Mark the Journey

    Save a moment, story, or lesson from your journey so it is not forgotten. Use the + Waymark button to return to the writer on your own Shelf; review your words before saving.

    “Today my daughter said something I never want to forget.”

  • Journal

    Thoughts & Reflections

    A place for longer thoughts that do not need to be polished. Select meaningful text to make a Waymark, a prayer, or a goal while keeping the original journal entry.

    Write freely first; decide what to carry forward afterward.

    Planned · More highlighted-text destinations are planned.

  • Captain’s Chart

    Purpose & Direction

    Write the goals, hopes, and intentions you want to live toward. When something happens along the way, tell that part of the story in a Waymark.

    The Chart is where you intend to go; a Waymark remembers what happened.

03

Wisdom

Wisdom holds what experience teaches you: a short truth, a fuller lesson, and guidance for how your family will live. Life Lessons and Family Rules are available now; a dedicated Golden Nuggets collection is planned.

  • Golden Nuggets

    Truth Worth Keeping

    A short truth you want to carry with you: a sentence, quotation, or insight that deserves to be remembered.

    “Being present matters more than being perfect.”

    Planned · A dedicated Golden Nuggets collection is planned. For now, keep the insight in a Waymark or Life Lesson.

  • Life Lessons

    Wisdom Worth Passing

    Explain what an experience taught you and why it matters. A lesson can link to an existing Waymark, giving your family the story behind the wisdom.

    “Here is what I learned about showing up when someone needs me.”

  • Family Rules

    Guidance Worth Keeping

    Put your shared values into words you can live by. These are the principles you want to practice and pass on—not just a list of things someone did wrong.

    “In our family, we apologize and make things right.”

04

Prayer & remembrance

Keep the prayer, remember the answer, and give special weight to the moments that changed your faith.

  • Secret Place

    Prayer With God

    Bring your prayers, burdens, and thanks before God. Return to a prayer as the story unfolds, and mark it answered when you want to remember His faithfulness.

    A prayer is the conversation; remembrance looks back on the answer.

  • Book of Remembrance

    Remember His Faithfulness

    Revisit prayers you have marked answered in the Secret Place. This is your record of answers you do not want to forget.

    Look back when you need encouragement to keep trusting.

  • 12 Stones

    Remember What God Did

    Set apart significant moments when God taught you, helped you, or carried you through. Mark a Waymark as a 12 Stones week to find it here again.

    Not every memory is a stone; choose the moments that anchor your faith.

    Planned · Directly turning an answered prayer into a linked 12 Stones memorial is planned.

Why This Exists

This whole app — every jar, every waymark, every prayer — is one thing: a commitment to live before the Lord. We number our days because they are not our own. They were given, and they will be accounted for. Before you record a single memory, stop and settle the one question that outweighs them all: are you right with God?

God made you, and God is holy

You are not an accident. You were made by God and for God, and He is holy — so holy that He cannot look upon sin.

“For all have sinned, and come short of the glory of God.” — Romans 3:23

Sin has separated us

Every one of us has broken His law — not in ignorance, but in rebellion. We have loved ourselves more than we have loved Him. And sin is not a small debt. It earns death, and a judgment beyond it.

“For the wages of sin is death; but the gift of God is eternal life through Jesus Christ our Lord.” — Romans 6:23

Read that again. Death is what we have earned. Eternal life is what God gives. You cannot work for a gift. You cannot buy it, or deserve it, or balance it out with good deeds. It is given, or it is not had at all.

Christ came, and Christ died, and Christ rose

Here is the gospel, the good news: while we were still His enemies, God did not wait for us to climb up to Him. He came down.

“But God commendeth his love toward us, in that, while we were yet sinners, Christ died for us.” — Romans 5:8

The sinless Son of God took upon Himself the wrath your sin deserved. He was crucified in your place, buried, and on the third day He rose again — death could not hold Him. The debt was paid in full by Someone who did not owe it.

“For God so loved the world, that he gave his only begotten Son, that whosoever believeth in him should not perish, but have everlasting life.” — John 3:16

Repent and believe

This is not a prayer you mumble to add heaven to a life that stays your own. Christ calls for two things, and they come together: repentance and faith.

Repentance is turning — a change of mind that becomes a change of direction. You turn from your sin and from being your own lord.

“Except ye repent, ye shall all likewise perish.” — Luke 13:3

Faith is casting the whole weight of your soul upon Christ alone — not on your goodness, not on your church, not on your effort. On Him.

“That if thou shalt confess with thy mouth the Lord Jesus, and shalt believe in thine heart that God hath raised him from the dead, thou shalt be saved. For whosoever shall call upon the name of the Lord shall be saved.” — Romans 10:9, 13

And know this, so that no pride creeps in: even the faith to believe is His gift, not your achievement.

“For by grace are ye saved through faith; and that not of yourselves: it is the gift of God: not of works, lest any man should boast.” — Ephesians 2:8–9

✦

Will you take this walk?

If God is stirring your heart even now, do not put it off. Today is the day. You do not need perfect words — He knows your heart. You can call on Him right where you sit. Something like this:

“Lord Jesus, I have sinned against You, and I cannot save myself. I turn from my sin. I believe You died in my place and rose again. Be my Lord and my Savior. Take my life — all my days — and let me live them before You from this moment on. Amen.”

If you have truly turned from sin and trusted in Christ, this is where your walk begins. Do not rest your assurance on the words of a prayer, but on Christ and His promises; let a life of continuing repentance, faith, and obedience bear witness to His work in you. This whole app is now yours: a life numbered, lived, and left before the Lord.

If you are turning to Christ, tell us. We want to rejoice with you, get you sound resources to grow, and — when we are able — send you a King James Bible of your own.

Email us — “I am beginning this walk”

Then find a Bible-believing church near you, be baptized, and do not walk alone.

Your prayers and journal entries inside can be protected by a screen lock.

What the Secret Place screen lock protects

It helps prevent casual viewing on this device. It guards everything inside the Secret Place — your prayers and your journal. It does not encrypt them, and cannot protect them from someone who has access to this browser’s stored website data.

Your passcode itself is never kept or synced in readable form. Only a scrambled fingerprint of it is stored, so the passcode cannot be read from this device or from the 90:12 servers.

The Secret Place relocks after five minutes without activity, when this page is hidden, and when the browser closes.
